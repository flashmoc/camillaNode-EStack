INPUT meters are raw capture metering and are intentionally read-only: reducing a digital gain after the ADC cannot repair ADC clipping. MASTER is the global CamillaDSP volume control and remains hard-limited to 0 dB. MID L/R and HIGH L/R gains are linked by default; mutes remain independent. Crossovers, delays, PEQ and protection filters are not changed here.
