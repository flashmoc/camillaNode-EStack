INPUT meters show the raw ADC/capture level, so they can reveal input clipping even when digital trim is reduced. IN L/R trims are linked by default and can be unlinked. MASTER is the global CamillaDSP volume control; E-Stack keeps its hard maximum at 0 dB. MID L/R and HIGH L/R gains are linked by default. Mutes remain independent. Crossovers, delays, PEQ and protection filters are not changed here.
